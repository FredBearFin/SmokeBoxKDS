# Try this first on your tablet

Ten steps, in order. Start from a cleared browser or a new private window. If something differs from "You should see", write down the step number and what you saw instead (a photo of the screen is best).

1. Open the Smokebox file. **You should see:** an empty board, "Welcome. Let's build your menu." and one big button "Add your first dish". No tickets, no red 86 count. *Report if:* anything is already filled in, or the big button is missing.
2. Tap **Add your first dish**. Type a dish name, press Enter (or Next on the keyboard), type a price like 12,50. **You should see:** "Reads as $12.50" under the price. *Report if:* the keyboard hides the box, or the price is read wrongly.
3. Tap where it is made, then **Save and add another**. **You should see:** "Saved ... at $12.50." and the name box empty with the same station still chosen. Add two more dishes the same way. *Report if:* any step needed a tap you did not expect.
4. Try a mistake: save with the name box empty, then with the price "abc". **You should see:** one plain red line each saying what to do. *Report if:* the words are unclear.
5. Tap **Save and finish**, then **New ticket**. **You should see:** your three dishes, nothing sold out. Tap two dishes, tap **Cash**, tap **Send to kitchen**. **You should see:** "Ticket #101 sent ... Paid ... by cash." and the ticket on the board. *Report if:* a dish is sold out or the toast does not appear.
6. Tap **Menu**, then **Tax, hours and PIN**. Set tax to 6 and save. **You should see:** "Saved. Orders add 6% sales tax." Send another ticket and look at the total. *Report if:* the total does not include 6%.
7. Close the browser tab, open the file again. **You should see:** the board, not a setup screen, with your dishes. Tap **Menu**, then **Dishes and prices** (two taps). *Report if:* anything was lost.
8. In **Dishes and prices**, change a price, tap away, then move a dish down with the arrow and tap **Undo**. **You should see:** "Saved." and the order change and change back. *Report if:* either does nothing.
9. Open **More ways to add dishes**, then **Have a menu file? Import it**. Paste `Latte 4.50` on one line and `Mocha` on the next, tap **Check it**. **You should see:** Mocha marked "no price", not ticked. Import Latte, then **Remove these 1**. *Report if:* Mocha could be imported without a price.
10. Tap **Menu**, then **Try the demo**, and read the bar across the top: "You are trying the demo. Nothing here is your kitchen." Tap **Back to my kitchen**. **You should see:** your own dishes unchanged and no demo bar. *Report if:* the bar is missing on any screen, or your dishes changed.

None of this has been tried on a real tablet yet; everything above was checked only in a desktop test browser.
