# Trying the demo

See Smokebox with food on it, without touching your own kitchen.

1. Open Smokebox with ?demo on the end of its address, like smokebox.html?demo.
2. It opens with sample dishes, three sample tickets and food in the cabinet. Cook, bump, pay and throw out food as much as you like.
3. Nothing you do in the demo reaches your own kitchen. The demo keeps its own saved data.
4. To go back, open Smokebox again without ?demo.

A backup made in the demo cannot be loaded into your own kitchen, and the other way round. Smokebox refuses it and changes nothing.

Sample numbers are made up. They are never your sales or your food.
