# Trying the demo

See Smokebox with food on it, without touching your own kitchen.

1. On the welcome screen, or in the **Menu**, tap **Try the demo**. Then tap **Try the demo** again to confirm. Tap **Not now** to stay where you are.
2. The demo opens with sample dishes, three sample tickets and food in the cabinet. A bar at the top says "You are trying the demo". Cook, bump, pay and throw out food as much as you like.
3. Nothing you do there reaches your own kitchen.
4. Tap **Back to my kitchen** on the bar to return. Your own dishes and orders are as you left them.

A backup made in the demo cannot be loaded into your own kitchen, and a real backup cannot be loaded into the demo. Smokebox refuses it and changes nothing.

The demo kitchen is a barbecue restaurant. Yours can be anything. Sample numbers are made up. They are never your sales or your food.
