# Troubleshooting

Plain fixes for sound, sleep, missing dishes and odd numbers. Lost data and the PIN are on the next page.

## No sound

1. If the board says "Tap here to turn on warning sounds", tap it. Browsers need one tap first.
2. Turn the tablet volume up and check its silent switch.
3. Tap **Menu** and check that Sound is on and the loudness is not low.
4. Tap **Menu**, then **Set up this tablet**, then **Play a test warning**.
5. If the bar says "Sound paused", it is outside your opening hours. Set the hours under **Tax, hours and PIN**.
6. Close any second Smokebox tab. Only the tab on screen makes sound.

## The screen went to sleep

Warnings cannot sound while the screen is off. Smokebox asks the browser to keep the screen on, but some browsers say no. Set the tablet to never sleep (Auto-Lock on an iPad, Screen timeout on Android) and keep it plugged in. When you wake it, check the times on the cards.

## A dish is missing

- Clear the search box and choose the All category on the New ticket screen.
- Open the **86 board**. A dish that is sold out or off the menu is listed there with the reason.
- Open **Dishes and prices** to see every dish you have added.

## A number looks wrong

- Check the date range and the filters at the top of the Back office page.
- Choose **Live only** to leave out the sample history, which is made up.
- Unpaid orders are not counted as money collected.
- Starter estimates (prep times, yields, costs) are guesses until you confirm them.
