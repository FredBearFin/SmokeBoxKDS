# Cabinet and warnings

For kitchens that cook food ahead, like pots of soup or trays of rice. Skip this page if you cook everything to order.

## Food in the cabinet

![Steps for a batch in the hold cabinet: it goes in, it is checked, portions are taken out, and it ends by being reheated or thrown out.](fig:cabinet "A batch in the hold cabinet.")

No **Cabinet** tab? Say yes to cooking ahead first (see Cooked-ahead food and portions).

1. Open the **Cabinet** tab. Tap **New batch**, choose the food, and enter the weight and a first temperature reading. Tap **Put in hold cabinet**.
2. Each batch has a card with one main button. Usually it is **Log temp**: take a reading at every check. A reading in range keeps the batch on the menu. When a batch is too cool the button becomes **Reheat**. Smokebox allows one reheat per batch. That is its own rule, not a health code rule. When it is past its best, it becomes **Keep serving**.
3. The **More for this batch** button (...) opens the rest: **Pull** as portions leave the cabinet, **Start cooling**, and **Discard**.
4. A batch out of time or out of range is thrown out: tap **Discard** and pick a reason.

## Warnings before the time runs out

![Four warning steps as a batch nears its time limit: a soft chime about an hour before, a voice call at thirty minutes, a loud repeating tone at ten minutes, and a different sound when time is up. A note says warnings only sound while the screen is on and Smokebox is open.](fig:ladder "The warning steps. Times shown are the usual ones.")

1. About an hour left: a soft chime, and a row appears in the strip at the top.
2. 30 minutes left: a voice call.
3. 10 minutes left: a loud tone and voice. It repeats every 20 seconds until you tap the strip row.
4. Time is up: a different sound, and the batch comes off the menu.

Some foods warn earlier. You can change the times in Settings. A batch that is almost empty, or on pace to sell out in time, stays quiet.

Warnings only sound while the screen is on and Smokebox is open on the board. They do not sound when the tablet is asleep, the page is closed, the volume is off, or it is outside your opening hours.
