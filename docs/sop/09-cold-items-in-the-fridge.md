# Cold items in the fridge

For cold food you keep in a fridge, like potato salad or a tray of dessert.

## Set it up

In **Dishes and prices**, add the item and choose Cold. Two optional boxes appear: how many days it may stay in the fridge, and how many hours it may be out of the fridge. Use the limits your local health department gives you. Leave a box empty and Smokebox keeps the time but says "No limit set". The limits are copied onto each batch when you make it.

## Each batch

Open the **Cabinet** tab and tap **New batch**. The card says "In the fridge since", shows the day it was made, and shows "Use by" with your number of days. The day it was made counts as day 1.

## Take out and Put back

Tap **Take out** when the food leaves the fridge for the line. The card says "Out of the fridge since" and the out-of-fridge clock runs. Tap **Put back** and the clock pauses. It adds up every time the food is out, and the card compares it with your hours, like "out of your 2 h limit". You are asked for your initials once, and an **Undo** bar follows each tap.

## The limits are yours

The days and hours are the numbers you typed. Smokebox does not know your local rules and does not say what they are. It only counts the time against your limits.
