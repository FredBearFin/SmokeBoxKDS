# Closing the day

A short routine for the end of service.

## Before you close

1. Finish the open orders. Tap **Orders**. For each open order tap Pay, or void it if the customer left.
2. Look in the **Hold cabinet**. Decide what to do with each batch that is left, using your own rules. To throw a batch out, tap **Discard** and pick a reason.
3. Wipe down food-contact surfaces and tap the **Sanitize** button on the board to log it.

## After you close

1. Look at the day. In **Back office**, open **Sales and money** for what you collected and **Safety** for the checks you logged and the food you threw out.
2. Save the log of thrown-out batches. Open it from the board's menu, then tap **Save as spreadsheet** or **Print or save as PDF**. Your health department may ask to see it.
3. Save a backup (see Backup and restore). After closing time Smokebox shows a bar with **Back up now**. It is a reminder, not a block.
