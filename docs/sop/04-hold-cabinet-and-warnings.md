# Hold cabinet and warnings

How cooked food is tracked, and how the board warns you before it runs out of time.

## Food in the hold cabinet

![Five steps for a batch in the hold cabinet: it goes in, it is checked, portions are taken out, and it ends by being reheated once or thrown out.](fig:cabinet "A batch in the hold cabinet.")

No **Cabinet** tab? Set Cooking ahead to Yes under **Tax, hours and PIN** (Menu, then Dishes and prices).

1. Open the **Cabinet** tab or the **Hold cabinet**. Tap **New batch** and enter the weight and a first temperature reading. Tap **Put in hold cabinet**.
2. Take a reading at every check with **Log temp**. A reading in range keeps the batch on the menu.
3. Tap **Pull** as portions leave the cabinet. The amount left goes down.
4. A batch that is too cool can be reheated once with **Reheat**.
5. A batch out of time or out of range is thrown out: tap **Discard** and pick a reason.

The cabinet tracks food by ingredient. If the Product list in New batch is empty, Smokebox has no ingredient to track yet.

## Warnings before the time runs out

![Four warning steps as a batch nears its time limit: a soft chime about an hour before, a voice call at thirty minutes, a loud repeating tone at ten minutes, and a different sound when time is up. A note says warnings only sound while the screen is on and Smokebox is open.](fig:ladder "The warning steps. Times shown are the usual ones.")

1. About an hour left: a soft chime, and a row appears in the strip at the top.
2. 30 minutes left: a voice call.
3. 10 minutes left: a loud tone and voice. It repeats every 20 seconds until you tap the strip row.
4. Time is up: a different sound, and the batch comes off the menu.

Some foods warn earlier. You can change the times in Menu and settings. A batch that is almost empty, or on pace to sell out in time, stays quiet.

Warnings only sound while the screen is on and Smokebox is open on the board. They do not sound when the tablet is asleep, the page is closed, the volume is off, or it is outside your opening hours.
