# Getting started

Add your dishes, send a test order, take payment and save your first backup. About ten minutes.

## 1. Add your first dish

1. A new Smokebox starts empty on purpose. Tap **Add your first dish** on the welcome screen.
2. Type what the dish is called and its price. Tap where it is made: Pit, Hot sides, Fry or Cold and drinks.
3. Tap **Save and add another** until your menu is in. Then tap **Save and finish**.

Change dishes and prices later: tap **Menu**, then **Dishes and prices**.

## 2. Set your tax and hours

Tap **Menu**, then **Tax, hours and PIN**. Enter your sales tax (0 if you add none) and your opening hours. A PIN is optional. It keeps line cooks out of the back office.

## 3. Send a test order

1. Tap **New ticket**.
2. Tap a dish, then tap **Send to kitchen**.
3. The ticket appears on the board. Tap **Start**, then **Done** on the dish, then **Bump** the ticket.

A test order is a real order in your reports. To remove it, void it (see Taking an order).

## 4. Take payment

Tap **Orders**. For cash, tap the Pay button on the order. It shows the total, like Pay $8.50 cash. For a card, a tip or a discount, tap **Tip or discount**, choose how it was paid, then tap **Mark paid**.

Smokebox only writes down how the order was paid. It never charges a card.

## 5. Save a backup

Tap **Back office**, then **Menu and settings**. Find Data, backup and reset and tap **Download backup (JSON)**. Keep a copy off this device: email it to yourself or put it on a USB drive.
