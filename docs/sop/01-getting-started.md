# Getting started

Set up the kitchen, send a test order, take payment and save your first backup. About ten minutes.

## 1. Set up your kitchen

On a new device the board shows a bar with **Set up your kitchen**. Tap it. It asks for your sales tax, your opening hours, an optional manager PIN, your dishes and any food that is already cooked. You can skip a step and come back to it later.

## 2. Add your first dish

1. Tap **Back office** at the top of the board, then **Menu and settings**.
2. Tap **Add a dish**.
3. Type the dish name and the price. Pick the station that cooks it.
4. Choose how it is cooked. One tap means it is done when it is plated, like a drink. Start, then done means the cook taps Start and then Done.
5. Tap **Add dish**.

Smokebox starts with sample dishes so you can practice. When you are ready for real service, tap **Use only my own dishes** on that same page.

## 3. Send a test order

1. Go back to the board and tap **New ticket**.
2. Tap your dish on the menu, then tap **Send to kitchen**.
3. The ticket appears on the board. Tap **Start**, then **Done** on the dish, then **Bump** the ticket.

A test order is a real order in your reports. To remove it, void it (see Taking an order).

## 4. Take payment

1. Tap **Orders**, find the order and tap **Pay**.
2. Choose Cash, Card or Other. Add a tip if there is one. Tap **Mark paid**.

Smokebox only writes down how the order was paid. It never charges a card.

## 5. Save a backup

Tap **Back office**, then **Menu and settings**. Find Data, backup and reset and tap **Download backup (JSON)**. Keep a copy off this device: email it to yourself or put it on a USB drive.
