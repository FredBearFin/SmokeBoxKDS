# Practice run and the demo

Learn Smokebox by doing, without touching your own kitchen.

## Practice run (2 minutes)

1. On the welcome screen, or in **Help**, tap **Practice run (2 minutes)**.
2. One practice ticket appears. A picture, a short line and a voice tell you each step, and a tapping finger shows the button. You tap Start, make one mistake on purpose and Undo it, tap Done, then Bump.
3. With no dishes yet, it runs in the practice kitchen. Tap **Now add my menu** at the end to go back.

## Watch a busy night

1. On the welcome screen, tap **Watch a busy night**. Then tap **Watch the demo** again to confirm. Tap **Not now** to stay where you are.
2. The demo opens with sample dishes, three fresh sample tickets and food in the cabinet, and it runs by itself: new orders arrive and a sample crew cooks and bumps them. A bar at the top says "Demo". Watch, or tap any dish to join in. A demo left open for hours starts fresh the next time.
3. Nothing you do there reaches your own kitchen.
4. Tap **Back to my kitchen** on the bar to return. Your own dishes and orders are as you left them.

A backup made in the demo cannot be loaded into your own kitchen, and a real backup cannot be loaded into the demo. Smokebox refuses it and changes nothing.

The demo kitchen is a barbecue restaurant. Yours can be anything. Sample numbers are made up. They are never your sales or your food.
