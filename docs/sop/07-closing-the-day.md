# Closing the day

Close the day when the last order is paid. It saves the day's totals and gives you a page to print.

## Close the day

1. Tap **Orders**, then **Close the day** at the bottom.
2. If an order is still open, it is listed. Every order must be paid or cancelled first. Tap **Open Orders to pay or void**, then come back.
3. If you cook ahead and food is left in the cabinet, choose **Keep it** (the timers keep running) or **Throw it all out** (logged as other).
4. Read Money today: cash, card and other. The cash line says how much should be in the drawer, on top of the cash you started with.
5. Tap **Close the day**.
6. Tap **Back up today's data now** and **Print today's summary**.

Closing does not lock anything. You can still take orders, and closing again later includes them. The app's day turns over at 4 am.

## Also

- Wipe down food-contact surfaces and tap **Sanitize** if you cook ahead.
- If your health department asks, save or print the thrown-out log. Open it from the **Menu** button on the board.
- Keep a copy of the backup off this device (see Backup and restore).
- For your accountant: in **Menu and settings**, under Spreadsheet files, tap **Sales by day (for your accountant)**. It saves one row for each day: sales, tax, tips and cash, card and other.
