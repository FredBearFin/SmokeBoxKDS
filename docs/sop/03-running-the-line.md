# Running the line

What a cook does on the kitchen board.

## Read the board

Each paper ticket is one order. Its clock counts up from when the order came in. It turns amber, then red, when the order is taking too long. The tabs at the top show one station. The All tab shows every ticket.

## Start and finish dishes

1. The line under the customer's name says what to do next.
2. Tap **Start** when you begin a dish that takes time. A countdown shows the usual cooking time.
3. When the countdown reaches zero the row says Check it. Look at the food. Smokebox never decides that a dish is done.
4. Tap **Done** when the dish is really finished. Quick dishes, like drinks, only need Done.

Gray dishes are waiting on purpose, so the whole order finishes together. Tap **Start now** to start one early.

## Send the ticket out

When every dish is done, tap **Bump**. If something is not done, Smokebox asks first. A ticket you bump by mistake can be brought back with **Undo** for 10 seconds, or later with **Recall**.

## When something runs out

Tap **86 board**, find the item and mark it sold out. It disappears from the order screen until you turn it back on. Smokebox also takes a dish off the menu by itself when its ingredient runs low.

## Food in the hold cabinet

Cooked food waits in the **Hold cabinet**. Log each batch with a temperature when it goes in, and again at every check. The board warns you before a batch reaches its serve-by time.
