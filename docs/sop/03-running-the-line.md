# Running the line

What a cook does on the kitchen board.

## Read a ticket

![A ticket on the kitchen board with seven numbered parts: ticket number and clock, customer, next step, a dish row, Start or Done, the more menu, and Bump.](fig:ticket "A ticket on the board. The numbers match the list below.")

1. Ticket number and clock. The clock counts up from when the order came in. It turns amber, then red, when the order is taking too long.
2. The customer, the order type and how many dishes are done.
3. The next step. This line says what to do next.
4. A dish. It shows its station, and a countdown once it is cooking.
5. **Start** or **Done** for that dish. Quick dishes, like drinks, only need Done.
6. The small menu: void the ticket or mark it as a rush.
7. **Bump** sends the ticket out when every dish is done.

## Start and finish dishes

1. Tap **Start** when you begin a dish that takes time. A countdown shows the usual cooking time.
2. When the countdown reaches zero the row says Check it. Look at the food. Smokebox never decides that a dish is done.
3. Tap **Done** when the dish is really finished.

Gray dishes are waiting on purpose, so the whole order finishes together. Tap **Start now** to start one early.

## Send the ticket out

If something is not done, Bump asks first. A ticket you bump by mistake comes back with **Undo** for 10 seconds, or later with **Recall**.

## When something runs out

Tap **86 board**, find the item and mark it sold out. Smokebox also takes a dish off the menu by itself when its ingredient runs low.
