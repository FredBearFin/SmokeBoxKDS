# Hold cabinet food and portions

Two ways to keep track of food you make before the order comes in.

## Count portions

Use this for a dish you make a batch of and sell until it is gone, like 20 sandwiches.

1. Tap **Settings**, then **Dishes and prices**. Under the dish, tap **I count what I made**.
2. Type how many you made and tap **Start counting**.
3. The dish shows how many are left, like "20 left". Tap **+10** or **Add** when you make more.
4. At zero the dish shows as sold out. **Stop counting** turns it off.

Counting does not track temperature or time.

## Track food you hold hot or cold

Use this for food kept in a warmer or cooler, like chili or potato salad, so Smokebox can warn you before it runs out of time.

1. In **Dishes and prices**, find **Hold cabinet food** and tap **Add cabinet food**. The Hold cabinet food sheet opens.
2. Type the food, like pulled pork or slaw, and choose Hot or Cold.
3. Set how long it may stay. Hot food takes one number of hours. Cold food has two optional boxes: days in the fridge, and hours out of the fridge. Use the limits your local health department gives you. Leave a box empty if none is set, and Smokebox will only show the time. See Cold items in the fridge.
4. Select the dishes that use it, and type the grams of it in one serving for each. Smokebox takes that much off the batch for each dish made.
5. Tap **Save cabinet food**. Then tap **Log a first batch**, or **Done for now**. To change an item later, open it from the list and tap **Save changes**.

The Hold cabinet is on from the start, because every kitchen holds some food hot or cold. A kitchen that truly cooks everything to order can turn it off in **Settings**, then **Tax, hours and PIN**, under **Hold cabinet**.
