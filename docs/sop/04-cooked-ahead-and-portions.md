# Cooked-ahead food and portions

Two ways to keep track of food you make before the order comes in.

## Count portions

Use this for a dish you make a batch of and sell until it is gone, like 20 sandwiches.

1. Tap **Menu**, then **Dishes and prices**. Under the dish, tap **I count what I made**.
2. Type how many you made and tap **Start counting**.
3. The dish shows how many are left, like "20 left". Tap **+10** or **Add** when you make more.
4. At zero the dish shows as sold out. **Stop counting** turns it off.

Counting does not track temperature or time.

## Track food you hold hot or cold

Use this for food kept in a warmer or cooler, like chili or potato salad, so Smokebox can warn you before it runs out of time.

1. In **Dishes and prices**, find "Do you cook any of this ahead and hold it?" and tap **Yes, I cook ahead**. Then tap **Add a cooked-ahead item**.
2. Type what you cook ahead and choose Hot or Cold.
3. Set how long it may stay. 4 hours is a careful starting point. Ask your health department and change it to match.
4. Select the dishes that use it. Smokebox takes about 150 grams off the batch for each dish made, a starting guess you can change.
5. Tap **Save cooked-ahead item**. Then tap **Log a first batch**, or **Done for now**.

Choose **No, skip this** if you cook everything to order. You can add cooked-ahead items later from the Cabinet.
