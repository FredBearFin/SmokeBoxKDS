# Who made this entry

Initials go next to temperature readings and thrown-out batches, so the log shows who did what. A wrong entry is corrected, not erased.

## Initials

1. The first time you log a temperature or throw a batch out on a tablet, Smokebox asks "Who is this?". Type your initials. **Skip** is allowed, and the action still goes ahead.
2. The tablet remembers them while you keep working, and asks again after two idle hours.
3. When someone else takes over, tap **Switch person** in the cabinet.

## Fix an entry

1. Open the thrown-out log from the board's menu. It says which period it covers.
2. Tap **Fix this entry** on the row.
3. Say what was wrong, add your initials and tap **Save the fix**.

The old entry stays in the log. Your fix is saved as a new entry next to it, with your initials and the time. That keeps the record honest.
