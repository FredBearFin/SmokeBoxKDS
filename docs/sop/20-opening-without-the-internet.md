# Opening without the internet (test feature)

A tablet can keep a copy of Smokebox so the page opens even when the network is down. This is a test feature, and nothing has been tried on real tablets.

## Does this tablet have a copy?

The card **Opens without the internet** in **Tablet checks: screen, sound, home screen** says whether this tablet keeps a copy of Smokebox. It works after you have opened Smokebox once with the kitchen connected. It needs a secure (https) address, or the hub computer itself. A plain http address on your network cannot do it. iPad Safari may clear stored data after about a week unless Smokebox is added to the home screen.

When a newer version is stored, Settings shows **A new version is ready**. Tap **Reload to update** when it is quiet; it never reloads by itself. If the copy misbehaves, tap **Reset the offline copy** in **Help and about**. Your board and orders are not touched.
