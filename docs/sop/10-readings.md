# Readings

Write down fridge, thermometer and sanitizer strip readings in one place.

## Open it

Tap **Settings**, then **Readings**.

## Three things you can write down

- **Fridge, freezer or display case**: pick which one, add a name if you like (like Walk-in), type the temperature, and tap **Log reading**.
- **Food thermometer check**: type what the thermometer says, say how you checked it (Ice water, Boiling water or Other), and tap **Log check**.
- **Sanitizer strip**: type the strip reading in parts per million. Add the sanitizer and where if you like. Tap **Log strip reading**. This also marks surfaces as sanitized.

Each entry goes in the safety record with your initials. Type the number the thermometer or strip really shows before you tap the log button.

## What it does not do

Smokebox writes down what you type. It does not know what a good reading is and cannot see the food. Use the numbers your health department and the sanitizer's label give you.

## Fix a wrong entry

Open the entry in **Recent readings** and tap **Save fix**. The old entry stays, with your fix next to it.

## The safety record

In **Back office**, open **Safety** and tap **Print the safety record**. It lists thrown-out batches, your readings and checks, and changes to limits, like "Changed the cold limit from X to Y". A cold reading says "above the cold limit" and how many minutes above it the batch has already used. The record says how long Smokebox keeps entries; ask your health department how long you must.
