# Empty, lost or locked

What to do when Smokebox opens empty, your data is gone, or you cannot get past the PIN.

## I closed the tab

Smokebox saves every change on this device as you go. Open it again the same way, in the same browser, and the board comes back. The clocks kept running while it was closed, and no warning sounded.

## The tablet was replaced or cleared

Your data lives only in the browser on one device. Clearing the browser data, using another browser, another file location or a private window shows an empty Smokebox. Restore your last backup (see Backup and restore).

## I forgot the PIN

Smokebox has no forgot-PIN button. The PIN is a door latch kept on this device, not real security. Five wrong tries lock the pad for 30 seconds.

The only way back in is to clear this site's saved data in your browser settings. That erases everything Smokebox saved on this device, including today's tickets and your menu. Do it only with a backup you can restore. A backup brings its PIN back, so use one made before the PIN was set.

## Smokebox opens empty

That is by design for a new Smokebox. It starts with no dishes, tickets or food in the cabinet, and it never guesses. Add dishes under **Dishes and prices**. If it was full yesterday, see "The tablet was replaced or cleared" above.

## What is saved where

- Tickets, dishes, settings and the PIN: in this browser on this device only.
- Sound, loudness, Day or Night and text size: on this device only.
- Other devices do not see your board. A backup file is the only copy you can move.
