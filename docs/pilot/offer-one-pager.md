# Smokebox for the pit: what you get (draft)

## What it does

- A tablet screen beside your pit. When you cook meat ahead and hold it, Smokebox tracks each batch and warns before it runs out of the time limit you set, with a chime, a voice, and a strip across the top of the screen.
- It keeps a log of what you throw out, with the reason and the cost, so you can see your waste.
- It takes orders and shows tickets to your cooks. It writes down how an order was paid.
- It starts empty and never guesses. You add your own dishes, or import a menu file. You can try a demo kitchen with made-up numbers and go back to your own.
- It works without internet. Your data lives on the tablet. You save a backup file and keep a copy somewhere else.
- It has a printed Guide inside the app: short pages and one-page cards for opening, service and closing.

## What it does not do

- It is **not a register or a POS**. It does not file taxes, run payroll or keep your books.
- It does **not charge cards**. Keep your card reader.
- It is **not a food-safety system and not a compliance guarantee**. It reminds you and keeps a record. You set the limits your local health department gives you, and your own rules and training come first. It cannot see or taste your food.
- It runs on **one tablet**. Two tablets do not share a board yet.
- Warnings only sound while the screen is on and Smokebox is open on the board. If the tablet sleeps, there are no warnings.
- Nothing has been tried in a real kitchen or on a real tablet yet. That is what the pilot is for. Keep your usual way of working beside it until you trust it.

## What the pilot asks of you

One real service with an observer standing in your kitchen who counts independently and does not help. About 15 minutes of setup beforehand. Your own system stays primary. At the end we ask what you throw out in a week and what you would pay.
