# Smokebox quick start

One page. For the owner or manager. The Guide inside the app (Settings, then Guide) has the details.

## Install

1. Open the Smokebox file in Chrome, Edge or Safari. It works without internet once it is open.
2. Add it to the home screen. On an iPad tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. In Chrome tap the menu, then Install app. Open it from that icon from now on.
3. Tap **Settings**, then **Tablet checks: screen, sound, home screen**. Work down the five checks. Set the tablet to never sleep and keep it plugged in.

## Your first 10 minutes

1. Tap **Add your first dish**. Type the name and the price, tap where it is made, tap **Save and add another**. Repeat, then **Save and finish**. Have a menu file? Use **Have a menu file? Import it**.
2. **Settings**, then **Tax, hours and PIN**: enter your sales tax and opening hours. A PIN is optional. If you set one, write down the recovery code it shows you.
3. Under your dishes, answer "Do you cook food ahead?". Answer **No** if you cook everything to order. **Yes, I cook ahead** adds the Cabinet, which keeps track of batches and warns you before time runs out.
4. Tap **New ticket**, tap a dish, tap **Send to kitchen**. On the board tap **Start**, then **Done**, then **Bump**. To remove a test ticket, use **Void ticket** in its small menu.
5. Take payment: tap **Orders**. Smokebox only writes down how an order was paid. It never charges a card.
6. Back up: tap **Back office**, **Menu and settings**, then **Download backup (JSON)**.

## When something goes wrong

| What happened | Do this |
|---|---|
| No sound | Tap the bar that says "Tap here to turn on warning sounds". Turn the volume up. Settings, **Tablet checks: screen, sound, home screen**, **Test the sound**. |
| Screen went to sleep | Warnings stop while it sleeps. Set Auto-Lock (iPad) or Screen timeout (Android) to Never. |
| Wrong tap | **Undo**, within 10 seconds. |
| Bumped by mistake | **Recall**, in the board header. |
| A dish is missing | Check the **86 board** and **Dishes and prices**. |
| Smokebox opens empty | Restore your last backup (below). It was not opened in the same browser, or the browser data was cleared. |
| Forgot the PIN | PIN pad, **Forgot the PIN?**, type your recovery code. |
| The tablet froze | Reload the page. Your orders are kept. |

## Where your backups are

Your kitchen is saved only in the browser on this tablet. A backup is a file, usually in the Downloads folder. Smokebox never sends it anywhere. Email it to yourself or put it on a USB drive, and keep a copy off the tablet. Back up every day at close.

To restore: **Back office**, **Menu and settings**, **Restore from a backup**, **Choose a backup file**, read the summary, then **Replace everything with this backup**.

## What Smokebox is not

It is not your register and it does not charge cards. It cannot see or taste food, and it does not know your local health rules: set the limits your local health department gives you. It runs on one tablet. Nothing has been tried in a working kitchen yet, so keep your usual way of working beside it until you trust it.
