# UX audit, 2026-10-23 (first pass)

Method: the merged build (`integration/all`) opened in headless Chromium at 1280x800, with the demo loaded, screenshots read by eye. Everything here is simulated in a browser; nobody has used it on a real tablet or in a real kitchen. This is a first pass, not the full audit: see "Not yet audited".

## Fixed in this pass
1. **Notifications blocked the top of the board.** The sound notice, the "Sound paused" note, two long strip rows and the welcome card took about 220 px of an 800 px screen. Now: every notice and strip row has a clear (X) button; a strip row only shows when its deadline is within the hour (or already bad, past or out) and is capped at two with one line of explanation; the closed-kitchen note only shows when something is due or sound is off; the welcome card is a slim bar. A cleared row comes back only at its next warning step, and clearing counts as seeing it (the loud tone stops). Far display keeps the roomy two-line version.
2. **The backup banner covered every sheet's title and close button** (Hold cabinet, Menu and others), because it sat above sheets. It now sits below them.

## Open, seen in screenshots (ranked)
1. **Toasts cover real content.** The "Busy-night demo started" toast sits over the first ticket, and "New tablet? Run the 6-step setup" sits over the Pace page. They can be closed, but info toasts should clear themselves after about 5 seconds and sit where they hide nothing.
2. **New ticket panel.** The customer chips are cut off by the footer and need scrolling; a toast overlaps the menu. The right-hand pane is too long for one screen at 800 px tall.
3. **Back-office filters eat the page.** Range, Day, Channel and Source take about 230 px before any number appears. They should fit one row.
4. **Hold cabinet cards are uneven.** The button grid differs from card to card, and each card is tall, so ten batches mean a lot of scrolling. "Starter stock" labels repeat on every card.
5. **Ticket slips are still tall** (a 3-item ticket is about 330 px), so only about four fit across at 1280 px.
6. **Demo markers repeat** ("Demo" on every ticket); fine for a demo, noisy if the demo stays on.

## Open, known from earlier work (not re-checked here)
- No screens yet for: close day, staff initials and safety corrections, the units setting (F/C, kg/lb).
- The hub is not connected to the browser app; devices do not share data.
- The slow browser test suites (`floor.e2e.mjs`, `shell.e2e.mjs`, `test/e2e/*`) were never run against the merged build and probably need updating for the empty start, the hidden "On time" badge and the new welcome button.
- The back-office PIN recovery path is unverified.
- Voice quality and audibility, wake lock, installing as an app, Safari and iPad: all untested on real hardware.

## Not yet audited
Orders sheet, the 86 board, Recall, Help, the New batch form, the Stations, Tickets, Sales and money, Safety, Stock and Data pages, role views (`?role=`), wall mode at 1920 and 2560, phone widths, Night theme, Large text. These need a second pass.

## Suggested order
Toasts (1), New ticket fit (2), back-office filter row (3), cabinet card layout (4), then a second audit pass of the unseen screens, then the slow test suites.
