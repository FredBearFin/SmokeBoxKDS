# Fifteen questions a first-time owner will ask

Plain answers that match what Smokebox does today. Where something is not decided or not built, the answer says so. The in-app Guide (Settings, then Guide) has the steps. See also docs/README-for-owners.md.

## 1. What if the internet goes out?

Nothing changes. Smokebox is one file and does not need the internet to take orders, show tickets, record payments or sound warnings. Your card reader and any delivery apps are separate and may stop working. Smokebox does not talk to them, so orders from a delivery app are typed in by hand, with or without internet.

## 2. What if the tablet dies, is lost or is wiped?

Your dishes, tickets, settings and PIN live only in that tablet's browser. If the tablet is gone and you have no backup file, they cannot be recovered. Save a backup every day at close and keep a copy off the tablet (email it to yourself or use a USB drive). With a backup, a new tablet is back to normal in a few minutes.

## 3. Can two tablets share the same orders?

Not yet. Each device has its own board, its own menu and its own tickets. Several tablets can share one kitchen through a separate program called the hub. That is a test feature that has not been tried on real tablets. Two tabs in the same browser on the same device stay in step, but two devices do not. Today, use one tablet for taking orders and showing the board.

## 4. Where does my data live?

In the browser on the device you use, nothing more. Smokebox does not send your orders, menu or sales to us or anyone else. A backup is a file you save and you decide where it goes. Clearing the browser's site data, or opening Smokebox in a different browser or a private window, shows an empty Smokebox.

## 5. How do I move to a new tablet?

Save a backup on the old one, copy the file somewhere safe, open Smokebox on the new tablet, then use Restore from a backup in the back office. Set up sound and keep-awake again on the new tablet, because those are per device. The Guide page "Replacing a device" has the steps.

## 6. What does it cost?

Not decided yet. There is no price to quote. Ask the person who set it up for you.

## 7. Can I rely on it for my food-safety records?

Treat it as a helper, not as your official record. Smokebox reminds you to take temperatures, warns before a time limit and flags readings outside the limits you set. It cannot see, taste or measure your food. The limits it starts with are examples, not your local rules, so set the limits your local health department gives you. Back office has a printable food safety record. Ask your health department whether they accept it before you stop your paper log. Until then, keep the paper log. Smokebox logs batches only, not cooler or freezer readings, sanitizer strength or thermometer checks, so keep paper logs for those too.

## 8. Does it charge customers' cards?

No. It only writes down whether an order was paid by cash, card or something else. Keep your card reader and your processor.

## 9. Has it been used in a real kitchen?

Not yet. It has been tested in a browser on a computer, not in a working kitchen or on a real tablet. Try it on your own tablet before a busy service and keep your usual way of working beside it until you trust it.

## 10. Do I have to cook the way the demo does?

No. The demo is a barbecue kitchen. A new Smokebox is empty. You add your own dishes, rename the stations, mark the ones you do not use and choose whether you cook food ahead. The Guide page "Setup recipes" shows four set-ups: counter cafe, food truck, restaurant with an expo, and cook-ahead.

## 11. I forgot my PIN. What now?

When you set a PIN, Smokebox shows a recovery code once. Keep it away from the tablet. On the PIN pad, Forgot the PIN? asks for the code and a new PIN. The PIN keeps cooks out of the back office and is not strong security. With no code, the only way in is to clear the site's saved data in the browser, which erases everything on that device, so do that only if you have a backup. A backup made after the PIN was set brings the PIN back.

## 12. Will I hear warnings when the tablet is asleep?

No. Warnings only sound while the screen is on and Smokebox is open on the board, with the volume up and within your opening hours. Set the tablet to never sleep and keep it plugged in. The Guide card "Tablet setup" lists the steps.

## 13. Can I bring in my menu from a spreadsheet or another system?

Yes, from a file. In Dishes and prices tap "Have a menu file? Import it". Smokebox shows what it understood and flags problems before adding anything, and "Remove these" takes an import back off. There is no live link to a register or delivery app.

## 14. How do I handle sales tax?

One flat percentage that you set under Settings, Tax, hours and PIN. Dish prices are before tax. It does not file taxes or handle different rates for different dishes.

## 15. Who do I ask when something goes wrong?

First, the Guide: Troubleshooting and the printable "When something goes wrong" card cover sound, sleep, missing dishes, lost data and the PIN. After that, ask the person who set Smokebox up for you. A support contact and response time for owners have not been decided yet.
