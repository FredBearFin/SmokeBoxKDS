# Smokebox: a short note for owners

## What it is

Smokebox is a kitchen screen and a simple order screen for a small restaurant or bar. Orders appear as tickets on a tablet, a computer or a TV. Cooks tap Start and Done. You can take payment, close the day and save a backup. It is one file, and it works without wifi or internet.

## What it is not

- It does not charge cards. It only writes down how an order was paid. Keep your card reader.
- It is not your register or your bookkeeping. It does not file taxes.
- It does not decide that food is safe. It reminds you to take temperatures and flags readings and times outside the limits you set. Your own food-safety plan and your local health code come first.
- It has been tested in a browser on a computer, not yet in a working kitchen. Try it on your own tablet before a busy service, and keep your usual system beside it until you trust it.

## Get started in three steps

1. Open Smokebox and tap **Add your first dish**. Add a few dishes with prices.
2. Tap **New ticket**, add a dish and tap **Send to kitchen**. Then tap Start, Done and Bump on the ticket, and take payment from **Orders**.
3. Save a backup: **Back office**, **Menu and settings**, **Back up now**. Do it every day at close.

The in-app **Guide** (Menu, then Guide) has short pages and printable checklist cards. Your data stays on the device unless you save or send a backup.

## Who to ask

Ask the person who set Smokebox up for you. A support contact for owners has not been settled yet (see docs/commercial-decisions.md, item 5). If something goes wrong first, the Guide's Troubleshooting page and its "When something goes wrong" card cover sound, sleep, missing dishes, lost data and the PIN.
